THE SYSTEM — a Solo Leveling style life tracker
================================================

Turn your real life into an RPG. Finish quests, earn XP and gold,
level up, climb the Hunter ranks, and spend your gold on rewards
you actually want.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Keep both files in the same folder.
2. Double-click "Launch THE SYSTEM.bat"  (or just open "THE SYSTEM.html").
3. A setup wizard runs the first time. It takes about two minutes:
     - your name and a motto
     - your six stats (pick presets or invent your own)
     - your daily quests
     - what goes in your reward shop
4. That's it. Start ticking things off.

No install. No account. No internet needed. It's one HTML file.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
DAILY QUESTS   Your everyday habits. Tick them for XP, stat points and gold.
               Starred ones (the star icon) are KEYSTONES — clear all of them
               each day or you fall into the Penalty Zone: lose XP, break your
               streak, and get the "Fatigued" debuff until you recover.

WEEKLY QUESTS  Bigger goals with a counter. Two extra "◆ THIS WEEK" focus
               quests rotate in automatically every Monday so it stays fresh.

MAIN QUESTS    Your long-term life goals, plus any personal objectives you add.

GATES          Turn a big scary project into a boss with an HP bar. Log effort
               to deal damage. Clear it for a big payout.

SHOP           Gold you earn buys the rewards you set up. Click any item to
               look it up online; hit the little flip button on its icon to see
               what it actually looks like.

SKILLS         Passive buffs unlock automatically as your stats grow. Active
               skills (double XP, triple gold, etc.) unlock by level and can be
               used once a week.

TITLES         Earned from feats. Equip one for a permanent buff.

SECRET QUESTS  Rare hidden trials that appear on their own after you complete
               something. Accept one and you're on the clock — clear it for a
               huge reward and an exclusive title, or fail it and take the hit.
               These are meant to be rare. Let them find you.

FREEZE         Going on holiday? Hit "❄ FREEZE". Nothing counts against you,
               your streak is held, and those days vanish from your stats.
               Hit "▶ RESUME" when you're back.


CHANGING THINGS LATER
---------------------
Hit "⚙ CUSTOMIZE" at the top right any time. You can rename your stats,
rewrite your daily quests, and add or remove shop rewards. Your level, gold,
streak and history are never touched.

You can also add/delete quests, goals and shop items directly in the app.


IMPORTANT — YOUR SAVE
---------------------
Your progress is stored in your browser, on this computer only.

  * Always open it the same way (the .bat, or always double-clicking the
    HTML). Opening it a different way can look like a blank save — your
    data isn't gone, the browser just files it separately.
  * Clearing your browsing data can erase it.
  * Use "⇩ EXPORT SAVE" now and then to download a backup file, and
    "⇧ IMPORT SAVE" to restore it (or to move to a new computer).

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. It's all yours, on your machine.


Now go level up.
